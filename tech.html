Safety & Security Infrastructure

Hardware SOS & Background Services

A dedicated hardware plugin runs background SOS services natively on Android and iOS. Physical coercion triggers offline-first SMS coordinate dispatch, completely bypassing the need for mobile data.

Decoy Generator & Duress PIN

Dual-PIN authentication. Entering a specific duress PIN opens a benign Decoy Vault populated by our Decoy Generator Service, protecting the victim during physical device seizures.

Alibi & P2P Transfer Services

Cryptographic hash validation for all media via our Alibi Service prevents evidence tampering. Secure P2P transfer and encrypted Cloud Backup services ensure data survives device destruction.
