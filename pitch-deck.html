THE CRITICAL REALITY: 64% of Bangladeshi women face online harassment; 80%+ cases go unreported due to panic deletion and legal intimidation.
SLIDE 02 • THE REALITY
The Problem: Online Violence Destroys Evidence & Silences Victims
Trauma, legal complexity, and physical coercion create an insurmountable barrier to justice.
🚨 1. Panic Deletion
Out of immediate terror or fear of family discovery, 80%+ of victims delete chat logs and block abusers, destroying vital bitwise proof before police can act.
Result: Case collapses under CrPC Sec 154 due to zero evidence.
⚖️ 2. Legal Intimidation
The Cyber Security Act has been repealed by the Cyber Protection Act 2026. Drafting an admissible General Diary (GD) is bewildering for traumatized victims.
Result: Duty Officers at local Thanas reject unstructured complaints.
🔍 3. The Forensic Gap
Simple screenshots are easily dismissed in court. Defense lawyers challenge them for lack of URLs, timestamps, or Evidence Act Sec 65B certificates.
Result: Evidence is ruled inadmissible by Divisional Cyber Tribunals.
📱 4. Physical Coercion
Abusive partners or family members frequently demand phones. An obvious anti-harassment app invites immediate domestic escalation and physical assault.
Result: Survivors are forced to delete safety tools or face violence.

SLIDE 04 • USER JOURNEY
How It Works: 5-Stage Journey From Chaos to Certitude
A seamless, trauma-informed pipeline turning traumatic harassment into courtroom-ready proof.
1. Capture
Survivor securely logs screenshot, voice note, URL, or chat export before blocking the abuser.
2. Authenticate
Protiti extracts RFC 3161 timestamps, EXIF tags, and generates bitwise SHA-256 cryptographic hashes.
3. Triage
Trauma-informed 4-step wizard gathers incident facts in plain, supportive Bangla without re-victimization.
4. Formulate
Engine auto-maps facts to CPA 2026 sections, generating DMP-standard General Diary payload.
5. Action
Survivor files online via Thana e-GD portal or dispatches encrypted dossier to BLAST/BNWLA lawyers.
BEFORE vs AFTER PROTITI:
BEFORE: Panic deletion → 0 legal proof → Duty officer rejection → Abuser impunity.
AFTER: Untouched encrypted vault → Bitwise SHA-256 verification → Evidence Act Sec 65B certified GD → Swift police action.
SLIDE 05 • ARCHITECTURE
Technology & Security: Built for Real-World Survival
Engineered to withstand physical coercion, rural network blackouts, and courtroom scrutiny.
🔐 Hardware Keystore & Duress Decoy PIN
• SQLCipher AES-256: Cryptographic keys stored inside Android Keystore / iOS Keychain.
• Physical Coercion Defense: Entering Master PIN 1234 unlocks genuine forensic evidence; entering Duress PIN 9999 displays an innocent student notebook with recipes.
• Zero Forensic Markers: Decoy mode completely conceals the existence of hidden forensic storage.
📲 Smart Calc Launcher Stealth
• OS Activity-Alias: Dynamically switches app icon and title to a working calculator on the home screen.
• Stealth Unlock: Typing 1971= opens the vault; typing 0000= opens decoy notes.
• Quick-Exit Gesture: Proximity/accelerometer face-down flip detection instantly wipes active RAM cache and restores calculator view in <300ms.
📡 Zero-Data SMS Fallback
• Offline Survivability: Compact GPS coordinates (<160 chars) transmitted over standard cellular SMS.
• Zero Internet Dependency: Operates seamlessly during rural data outages, credit exhaustion, or mobile throttling.
• Emergency USSD Code: Quick broadcast protocol for feature phone cross-compatibility.
⚖️ Evidence Act Sec 65B Admissibility Engine
• NIST FIPS 180-4 Hashing: Produces cryptographic SHA-256 fingerprints the instant evidence is ingested.
• Automated Sec 65B Certificates: Generates court-admissible electronic authenticity certificates conforming to the Evidence (Amendment) Act 2022.
• Forensic Chain of Custody: Adheres to CID Forensic Lab evidentiary protocols.
