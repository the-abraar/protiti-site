A Digital Evidence Vault app providing a frame of safety to structure the path to justice. Protiti empowers women facing online harassment in Bangladesh by securely documenting and formatting evidence for direct legal action.

See How it Works Under the Hood

Chaos The Reality

64% of Bangladeshi women face digital violence. Victims are silenced by an overwhelming lack of structure and social stigma.

  • ✗ Evidence Loss: Victims often delete messages out of fear, destroying crucial proof.
  • ✗ Legal Complexity: Filing a General Diary (GD) and navigating the Cyber Protection Act, 2026 is intimidating.
  • ✗ Metadata Gap: Screenshots are dismissed because they lack timestamps, URLs, and required metadata under the Evidence Act.

Clarity The Protiti Solution

We shift the burden of proof from the victim's trauma to undisputed certitude.

  • ✓ Zero-Knowledge Security: End-to-end encryption with local SQLCipher storage and encrypted cloud backups.
  • ✓ Legal Logic Engine: Our Complaint Wizard directly maps incident data to the Cyber Protection Act, 2026 and Bangladesh Police e-GD schema.
  • ✓ Resilience: Offline-first design with a dedicated hardware SOS plugin and P2P secure transfer.

The Architecture of Safety

📦

Secure Evidence Vault

Encrypted SQLCipher storage. Features a Duress PIN that opens a Decoy Vault (powered by our Decoy Generator Service) for physical coercion scenarios.

📝

GD-Automator Wizard

Guided by Jev AI, it structures evidence (with cryptographic Alibi Service metadata) into actionable formats ready for Police API integration.

🚨

Panic Frame & Background SOS

A native hardware SOS plugin sends offline-first SMS coordinates and direct alerts to PCSW in severe danger.

🤝

Support Bridge

Connects victims with vetted pro-bono lawyers (BLAST, BNWLA) and mental health counselors via secure P2P transfers.

Target Community & Impact

Designed for female university students (18-30) facing digital harassment. Implemented through a phased 8-month roadmap, including pilot testing in home divisions and campus workshops.
